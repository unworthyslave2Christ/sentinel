NORTHSTAR COMMUNITY SERVICES
PROCUREMENT PROCEDURE

Document Type: PROCEDURE
Document ID: NCS-PRO-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 1 January 2026
Process Owner: Procurement Manager

1. PURPOSE
This Procedure describes the steps staff must follow when acquiring goods or services on behalf of Northstar Community Services.

2. STEP 1 - IDENTIFY THE REQUIREMENT
The requesting department shall define the business need, required specifications, estimated cost, and desired delivery date.

3. STEP 2 - OBTAIN APPROVAL
The requester shall obtain the required budget and management approval before committing organizational funds.

4. STEP 3 - IDENTIFY SUPPLIERS
The Procurement Team shall identify suitable suppliers using approved supplier records, market research, or a competitive quotation process where required.

5. STEP 4 - EVALUATE QUOTATIONS
Quotations shall be evaluated against relevant criteria such as price, technical suitability, delivery time, service capability, and contractual terms.

6. STEP 5 - SELECT SUPPLIER
The authorized approver shall confirm the supplier selection and document the basis for the decision.

7. STEP 6 - ISSUE PURCHASE DOCUMENT
A purchase order or other approved purchasing document shall be issued before the supplier begins work, unless an approved exception applies.

8. STEP 7 - RECEIVE AND VERIFY
The requesting department shall verify that delivered goods or services meet the agreed requirements.

9. STEP 8 - PAYMENT
Invoices shall be checked against the purchase documentation and evidence of receipt before payment is authorized.

10. RECORD KEEPING
Procurement records shall be retained in the approved procurement repository.

11. ESCALATION
Unusual purchases, conflicts of interest, suspected supplier misconduct, or material deviations from approved requirements shall be escalated to the Procurement Manager.

12. REVIEW
This Procedure shall be reviewed annually or when the procurement process materially changes.
