NORTHSTAR COMMUNITY SERVICES
INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Document Type: POLICY
Document ID: NCS-POL-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 1 January 2026
Policy Owner: Director of Operations

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes the basic information security requirements for Northstar Community Services and its personnel.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to employees, contractors, volunteers, temporary workers, organizational devices, information systems, and information handled on behalf of the Organization.

3. ACCESS CONTROL
Access to organizational systems shall be granted according to job responsibilities. Users shall:
- Use individual accounts where available.
- Keep authentication credentials confidential.
- Use multi-factor authentication when provided.
- Request removal or modification of access when responsibilities change.

4. INFORMATION HANDLING
Confidential and sensitive information shall be stored and transmitted using approved organizational systems. Personnel shall not intentionally disclose protected information to unauthorized persons.

5. DEVICES
Organizational devices shall use reasonable security controls, including supported operating systems, screen locking, and approved security software.

6. INCIDENT REPORTING
Suspected unauthorized access, data loss, malware, or other security incidents shall be reported to the Operations Manager as soon as reasonably possible.

7. THIRD-PARTY ACCESS
Third parties shall receive only the access necessary to perform authorized services. Where appropriate, contractual confidentiality and security requirements shall be established.

8. POLICY COMPLIANCE
Personnel are expected to comply with this policy. Violations may result in corrective action in accordance with applicable organizational procedures.

9. REVIEW
The Policy Owner shall review this policy at least annually or following a significant security event or organizational change.

Approved by: Executive Director
Approval Date: 1 January 2026
