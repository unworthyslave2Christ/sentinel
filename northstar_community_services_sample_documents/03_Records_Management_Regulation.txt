NORTHSTAR COMMUNITY SERVICES
RECORDS MANAGEMENT REGULATION

Document Type: REGULATION
Document ID: NCS-REG-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 1 January 2026
Issuing Authority: Executive Director

1. PURPOSE
This Regulation establishes mandatory organizational requirements for the creation, classification, retention, protection, and disposal of official records.

2. OFFICIAL RECORDS
Official records include approved contracts, policies, financial records, meeting minutes, operational reports, personnel records, and other documents designated as organizational records.

3. CLASSIFICATION
Records shall be classified as:
- Public: information approved for general distribution.
- Internal: information intended for authorized organizational use.
- Confidential: information requiring restricted access.
- Restricted: information requiring heightened protection because of its sensitivity.

4. RETENTION
Records shall be retained for the period specified in the Organization's approved records schedule. No record subject to an active legal, audit, or investigative hold shall be destroyed.

5. STORAGE
Official electronic records shall be stored in approved organizational repositories. Physical records shall be stored in locations protected from unauthorized access, loss, and avoidable damage.

6. DISPOSAL
Records reaching the end of their approved retention period may be securely destroyed, subject to applicable holds and authorization requirements.

7. RESPONSIBILITIES
Department heads are responsible for ensuring that records created by their departments are properly maintained. The Records Officer shall provide guidance and monitor compliance.

8. EXCEPTIONS
Any exception to this Regulation must be documented and approved by the Executive Director or an authorized delegate.

9. ENFORCEMENT
Failure to comply may result in corrective action under applicable organizational procedures.

Issued by: Office of the Executive Director
