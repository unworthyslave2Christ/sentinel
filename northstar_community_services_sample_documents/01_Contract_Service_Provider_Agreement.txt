NORTHSTAR COMMUNITY SERVICES
SERVICE PROVIDER AGREEMENT

Document Type: CONTRACT
Document ID: NCS-CON-001
Effective Date: 1 January 2026
Review Date: 1 January 2027

PARTIES
This Service Provider Agreement is between Northstar Community Services ("the Organization") and BrightPath Technology Solutions ("the Service Provider").

1. PURPOSE
The Organization engages the Service Provider to provide website maintenance, technical support, and routine system administration.

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES
The Service Provider shall:
- Maintain the Organization's public website.
- Provide technical support during agreed service hours.
- Apply approved security and software updates.
- Maintain appropriate records of support activities.
- Notify the Organization promptly of significant service interruptions.

3. TERM
This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and remains in force for twelve months unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.

4. FEES AND PAYMENT
The Organization shall pay the Service Provider a monthly service fee of 250,000 fictional currency units. Approved additional work shall be agreed in writing before commencement.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties shall protect confidential information received in connection with this Agreement and shall use such information only for legitimate business purposes.

6. PERFORMANCE
The Service Provider shall perform the services with reasonable care, skill, and professionalism.

7. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty days' written notice. Immediate termination may occur where a material breach is not corrected within a reasonable period after written notice.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This fictional Agreement is governed by the laws selected by the parties in their final executed version.
