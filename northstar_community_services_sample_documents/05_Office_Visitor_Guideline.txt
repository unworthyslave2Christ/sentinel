NORTHSTAR COMMUNITY SERVICES
OFFICE VISITOR GUIDELINE

Document Type: OTHER
Document ID: NCS-OTH-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 1 January 2026

1. PURPOSE
This document provides practical guidance for welcoming and managing visitors at Northstar Community Services offices.

2. BEFORE THE VISIT
Where practical, employees should notify reception or the designated office coordinator when expecting a visitor. The visitor's name, organization, host, and expected arrival time should be provided.

3. ARRIVAL
Visitors should report to reception upon arrival. Reception personnel may request identification or basic visit information when appropriate.

4. HOST RESPONSIBILITY
The employee hosting a visitor should meet the visitor at reception and remain responsible for the visitor during the visit.

5. RESTRICTED AREAS
Visitors should not enter restricted work areas, storage rooms, server rooms, or other controlled spaces unless specifically authorized and accompanied.

6. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Employees should take reasonable steps to prevent visitors from viewing confidential documents, computer screens, or conversations that are not intended for them.

7. DEPARTURE
Visitors should return temporary access items and sign out where a visitor register is maintained.

8. EMERGENCIES
During an emergency, visitors should follow instructions from designated safety personnel and remain with their host when it is safe to do so.

9. CONTACT
Questions about this guideline should be directed to the Office Coordinator.

This document is operational guidance and does not replace a formal organizational policy, regulation, contract, or procedure.
